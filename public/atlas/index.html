About the data and coverage

Each red shape is an agency-reported fire perimeter recorded in the National Fire Database (NFDB), selected by jurisdiction for the 1980–2024 view. A pale grey map is not evidence that an area has never burned: perimeter coverage varies by place and year. The NFDB polygon archive has no Nunavut agency layer; only any Parks Canada perimeters that intersect Nunavut can appear there. New Brunswick and Newfoundland and Labrador have substantial missing recent years; Prince Edward Island has only 2024 polygons. These maps show recorded footprints, not fire counts or probabilities.

Population-centre names and 2021 population rankings come from Statistics Canada. The images are bundled with this website, so zooming works without a live map service. Read the method, coverage notes and source attribution, or open the manifest.
